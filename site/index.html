Install in three steps

Takes about a minute.

  1. 1

    Drag to Applications

    Open Uncloud.dmg and drag Uncloud to Applications.

  2. 2

    Open Uncloud

    Open Uncloud from Applications. The first time, if macOS says it can’t be opened, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

  3. 3

    Sign in to iCloud

    Sign in to iCloud Photos and choose your backup drive. No Uncloud account needed.

Questions

Why does macOS warn me the first time?

This test build isn’t notarized by Apple yet. Use Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security once.

Is it free?

Yes. Uncloud is free, every feature is included, and there’s no account to create.

Can Uncloud see my photos?

No. Photos go straight from Apple to your drive. They never pass through us.

Will it delete anything from iCloud?

Only if you ask. Export never deletes. Clean Up iCloud asks first, and deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

My library is huge. Will it cope?

Yes. It works in steps, resumes after breaks and skips what’s already done.
