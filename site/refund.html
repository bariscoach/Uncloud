Refund Policy

Effective: November 26, 2026

This policy covers one-time Uncloud licenses sold at www.tryuncloud.com: Plus for $29 (1 Mac), Pro for $49 (2 Macs), and Family for $69 (5 Macs). They are not a subscription. You do not need an Uncloud account to buy a license, to use one, or to ask for a refund.

How to request a refund

If you are not satisfied, email barishiz@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase. Include the email address you used at checkout, and the license key if you have it. We will refund you in full to the original payment method, through our checkout provider.

What happens to the license

Once a refund is issued, the license key is revoked and can no longer be used on any Mac. A purchase does not create an Uncloud account, so there is no account to cancel.

After 14 days

After 14 days, we generally do not offer refunds, except where the law requires it. If something is wrong with the key or the purchase itself, email us and we will help.

Payment

Payment is processed by Stripe. We do not receive your card number. The price you pay is the one-time price shown on the pricing page at checkout.

Contact

Refund questions: barishiz@gmail.com
