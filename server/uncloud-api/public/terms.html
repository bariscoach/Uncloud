Last updated: October 23, 2026

The service

Uncloud is a Mac app that exports your photos from iCloud to a drive you choose, keeps automatic backups, lets you browse your backup by date, place and map, and can delete photos from your iCloud library when you confirm.

Free, with an account

Uncloud is free. Every feature is included. To use it, create an Uncloud account with your email address in the app. There is nothing to pay.

Your account

Keep your sign-in email private. You can delete your account in the app at any time. Deleting your account removes your account record. It does not delete any photos on your Mac, your backup drive or your iCloud library.

Your photos

Your photos go straight from Apple to your drive. They never pass through Uncloud’s servers. You’re responsible for keeping your backup drive safe.

Deleting from iCloud

Deleting from iCloud removes photos from your iCloud library only when you confirm. Recently Deleted keeps them for up to 30 days.

No warranty

Uncloud is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, we aren’t liable for lost data or indirect damages.
