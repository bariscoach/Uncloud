The service

Uncloud is a Mac app that exports your photos from iCloud to a drive you choose. Export is free. Uncloud Plus adds automatic backups, reminders, Browse Backup, Find Duplicates, Verify Backup, Free Up Space and Clean Up iCloud.

Renewal

If you choose to renew automatically, your plan renews every year at the then-current price, and we remind you by email before it does. You can turn renewal off anytime in Uncloud › Settings › Account or in the billing portal; your plan then runs to the end of the year you paid for. If you choose one year with no auto-renewal, you won’t be charged again unless you turn renewal on.

Deleting from iCloud
