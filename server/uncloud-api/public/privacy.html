Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

What we store

Your email address and the account record Supabase keeps for sign-in. Nothing else.

What we never see

Your photos, your Apple ID password and your iCloud data. Photos go straight from Apple to your drive.

Who processes it

Supabase (account sign-in and storage), Vercel (hosting this service) and, later, Resend (sending sign-in codes).

Launch list

If you join the launch list on our website, we keep your email address and the date you joined. We use it only to tell you when Uncloud launches. We don’t share it. Email barishiz@gmail.com and we’ll delete it.

Emails

We send sign-in codes. No marketing.

Deleting your account

Use Delete Account in the app, or email barishiz@gmail.com, and we delete your account record and your sign-in record.
