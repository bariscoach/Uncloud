Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

What we store

Your email address, a Stripe customer ID, and your plan’s status and renewal date. Nothing else.

What we never see

Your photos, your Apple ID password and your iCloud data. Photos go straight from Apple to your drive.

Who processes it

Supabase (account sign-in and the plan database), Stripe (payments, receipts and tax), Vercel (hosting this service) and, later, Resend (sending sign-in codes). Card details go to Stripe only.

Launch list

If you join the launch list on our website, we keep your email address and the date you joined. We use it only to tell you when Uncloud launches. We don’t share it. Email [support email] and we’ll delete it.

Emails

We send sign-in codes and, through Stripe, receipts and renewal reminders. No marketing.

Deleting your account

Email [support email] and we delete your account and plan record. Stripe keeps payment records as tax law requires.
